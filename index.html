## Background

was a HUMSS student in Senior High School at Albor National High School in the Dinagat Islands. Originally, Information Technology (IT) was not my main choice because I wanted to pursue Criminology to become a police officer. However, we faced many financial difficulties regarding tuition. As the middle child, I always worried about whether my parents could afford my college education. The interesting part is that I did not take Criminology because my father suggested that IT is a better choice since it is highly in demand today.

## What I'm focused on

Right now I'm deepening my front-end fundamentals while getting comfortable with back-end basics (databases, simple APIs). I like projects with a visible, usable result — I'd rather ship something small and working than plan something huge and unfinished.

## Goals

Land an internship where I can work on a real codebase with other developers, and eventually specialize in either full-stack web development .
